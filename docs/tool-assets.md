New Survival and Sandbox runs have one hand saw and one crowbar in backpack storage. The crowbar is also the selected melee weapon: its gear slot refers to that same inventory item, without adding a duplicate or charging weight twice. Legacy saves retain their prior bat selection until a crowbar is equipped. The crowbar has a curved 3D steel model using the existing two-handed attack rig.

Seven recipes fill basic toolkit gaps: hand saw, crowbar, hammer, axe, wrench, fishing rod and nails. Arsenal links directly to tool recipes; workbench context menus expose them too. Basic hammer and saw recipes have no tool or workbench prerequisite.
